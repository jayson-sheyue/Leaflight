# Leaflight v0.12.1 — Full window without standing buttons

- Mac and Windows 0.12.1 keep full-window reading, writing, and review. The sidebar, page header, and system menu bar hide. Inbox and Exit are not standing buttons. They appear briefly at the top right after entering full window or tapping the page, in landscape and portrait, then hide. Esc restores the previous layout and keeps the current reading text.
- The v0.12.0 DMG and Windows installer still show two standing controls. Download this release instead.
- iPhone and iPad use the [web preview](https://jayson-sheyue.github.io/Leaflight/). There is no iOS installer.
