# Leaflight v0.12.3 — Spine-hinged physical paper curl

- Refined the **Paper · 右下角卷页** (`paper`) page-turning effect on Mac, Windows, and the web preview so it turns like an inextensible physical book page anchored along the left spine instead of sliding a detached white oval tube off the top-left corner.
- Turning forward lifts the bottom-right corner smoothly, reflects the reverse side of the sheet with subtle foreshortening and faint ink bleed-through, keeps the diffuse paper-roll highlight clipped strictly inside the folded sheet, and casts directional overhang, valley, and spine shadows onto the flat pages across all 10 light and dark themes.
- Turning backward (`Previous page`) unfolds the previous sheet back from the left spine onto the right edge rather than peeling the bound left margin open.
- Android Reader 0.3.0-preview.8 shares the same `ReaderTransition` implementation and receives the identical paper-curl update.
- iPhone and iPad use the [web preview](https://jayson-sheyue.github.io/Leaflight/). There is no iOS installer.
